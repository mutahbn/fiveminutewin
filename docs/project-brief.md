# The Daily Win — Project Brief

*Working title. Domain to be confirmed. Prototype review copy — July 2026*

## What this project is

The Daily Win is a website that teaches everyday people how to use AI — not by explaining it, but by having them use it. Every day, the site publishes one small "mission": a real-life task the visitor completes with the help of any free AI chatbot in about five minutes. Examples: sending a difficult email they've been avoiding, planning the week's dinners from what's in the fridge, negotiating a lower phone bill, decoding a confusing insurance letter.

The visitor leaves each day with something actually done in their real life — a win — not just something learned.

## Who it's for

People who are not technical but keep hearing they should "learn AI": parents, small-business owners, job-seekers, retirees. They don't want courses, jargon, or theory. They want AI to be useful to them today.

## How it works

1. **Daily mission** — one 5-minute real-life task, with the exact prompt to copy and a plain-language explanation of *why* it works.
2. **Personas** — the same mission is rewritten for four different lives (shop owner, parent, job-seeker, retiree). The visitor picks theirs and every example changes to match.
3. **The Daily Challenge** — instead of a quiz, a 30-second game: two prompts, guess which produced the better result, then see why. Designed to be shareable, like a daily puzzle.
4. **Streaks and wins** — each completed mission is logged; visitors build a streak and a growing record of real things AI helped them accomplish.
5. **Membership** — the daily content is free forever. Paid members get a permanent "Life Portfolio" of their wins, an "AI-Ready" certificate after 30 wins, and the ability to submit their own real-life problems to become future missions.

## What it seeks to accomplish

- Make AI genuinely useful to non-technical people, one small real-life task at a time — a meaningful contribution, not another hype site.
- Build a daily habit (like Wordle or Duolingo) so users return every day, driven by streaks, the challenge game, and real-life payoff.
- Grow globally through word of mouth: shareable challenge scores and stories like "this site saved me $18 a month."
- Generate sustainable revenue through an affordable membership — without ever paywalling the core daily lesson.

## What makes it different

Competing sites explain AI (articles, videos, courses); knowledge fades because nothing is applied. Here the lesson *is* the application. The measure of success is not "did the user understand?" but "did the user actually do something useful today?"

## How to evaluate the prototype

The prototype is a clickable single-page demo (four screens: Home, Today's Mission, The Challenge, Membership). It contains placeholder content and simulated data. Judge it on these questions:

1. **Clarity** — Would a non-technical 55-year-old understand, within 30 seconds of the homepage, what this site does and why it's for them?
2. **Relatability** — Do the mission scenarios and personas feel like real life, not tech examples? Does switching personas noticeably change the experience?
3. **Engagement** — Is the challenge game fun enough to play daily and share? Does the streak/win mechanic create a reason to come back tomorrow?
4. **Usefulness** — Could someone copy the provided prompt, use it in a real AI chat today, and get a real result? Does the "why this works" note actually teach?
5. **Distinctiveness** — Does this feel different from typical AI tutorial sites and online courses?
6. **Membership appeal** — Does the free/paid split feel fair, and would the member benefits (portfolio, certificate, submitting problems) plausibly be worth ~$5/month?
7. **Look and feel** — Does the design feel warm, human, and trustworthy rather than technical or corporate?

A good verdict answers: which of these seven the prototype achieves, which it misses, and what single change would most improve it.
